Privacy Policy

Masarif for Android · Published by Masarif Finance · Effective 3 October 2026

In short. Masarif keeps your financial records on your phone. We run no servers, you create no account, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Information leaves your phone only when you choose an action that sends it, such as pushing a statement to your own Google Sheet, sending a WhatsApp reminder, or sharing an export. Those actions are listed below.

Who we are

Masarif ("the app") is published by Masarif Finance ("we", "us"). You can reach us at alizubair686@gmail.com.

Information the app stores on your phone

Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Transactions: titles, amounts, dates, items, budget categories and recurring schedules.
  • Budgets and their limits.
  • Assets and their recorded values, and automatic contribution settings.
  • Loans: the person's name, an optional phone number, amounts, due dates and repayments.
  • Money held for others: the person or fund name, notes, entries and items, and an optional Google Sheet link you provide.
  • Zakat estimate settings.
  • App settings: language, alerts, and app lock. If you set a passcode, it is kept in your device's secure storage.

We cannot see any of this. It is not sent to us, because the app does not connect to any service operated by us.

How the stored information is protected

The records are kept in a database inside the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a standard, unmodified Android device. The app does not add its own encryption to this database; it relies on the protection Android and your device provide, including your screen lock and device encryption. You can add an app lock with a passcode and, if you choose, fingerprint or face unlock.

Backups you create in the app are encrypted with a recovery passphrase that you choose. CSV and PDF exports are not encrypted.

When information leaves your phone

Only through these actions, each started by you:

ActionWhat is sentWho receives it
Push to Google Sheet (money held for others)That one person's or fund's name, purpose, entries, items and balanceGoogle, into a Google Sheet in your own Google account, through a script link you set up
WhatsApp loan reminderThe reminder text, including the person's name, the amount and any due date, and the phone number you savedWhatsApp, on your phone. Nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp

Information handled by Google, WhatsApp or your device's speech service is subject to their own privacy policies. If you share a Google Sheet with someone, they can see what is in that sheet.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Contacts (optional): only when you tap "Contacts" on a loan, to let you pick one person. The app saves that person's name and number with the loan on your phone. It does not copy your address book, upload contacts, or change them.
  • Microphone (optional): only while you dictate a transaction. The app does not keep audio recordings.
  • Notifications (optional): to show budget alerts created on your device.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock (optional): to unlock the app. Your device performs the check and tells the app only whether it succeeded; the app never receives biometric data.
  • Internet: used for the Google Sheet push and, if your device's speech service needs it, for dictation.

You can refuse or withdraw any optional permission in Android settings and keep using the rest of the app.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share your personal or financial information.
  • We do not show advertising or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting or tracking tools.
  • We do not read your SMS messages or connect to your bank.

Information about other people

You may record details of other people, such as someone you lent money to or a relative whose money you hold. You are responsible for having a proper reason to keep that information and for whom you share it with, for example through a Google Sheet or a WhatsApp message.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your records stay on your phone until you delete them. You can delete individual records in the app. Clearing the app's data in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device. This cannot be undone unless you kept a backup.

Copies you have sent elsewhere, such as a Google Sheet, a shared export or a WhatsApp message, are outside the app. Delete those where they are kept. Because we hold none of your information, we have nothing to delete or hand over on request, but you are welcome to contact us with any question.

Children

Masarif is a finance tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches you through an app update.
